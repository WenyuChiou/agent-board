- **Pull requests from outside the team get their own caps and claim rules, and a small docs fix needs no issue (#482).** The PR check reads the author's `author_association` and login from the event and the open pull request list (a local `--body-file` run counts as the team). An outside pull request is held to two open per login and four in all, counted apart from the team's eight; the governance and `cap-exempt` exemptions do not apply to it. Each issue it names needs a `Claim:` comment from the author's own login that names the branch, the labels `ready` and `for-agent` and not `blocked`, and no live claim from another login; a `chore(release):` title fails. A change of more than 500 lines or 15 files, or to `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `.github/`, `package.json` or `.claude-plugin/`, is only noted. For every author, a `docs:` pull request that changes only `README*.md` or `docs/**/*.md` (not `docs/FORMAT.md` or `docs/dev/`) in at most three files and 50 added lines needs no issue line and no claim. CONTRIBUTING.md gains the Outside contributors section.
  Tests: `pr-check counts an outside author's open pull requests by login, apart from the team's eight`; `pr-check fails an outside pull request whose claim was written by someone else or whose issue is not ready`; `pr-check lets a small docs pull request from an outside author through without an issue or a claim`; `pr-check only notes, and does not fail, a large or governance-touching outside pull request`; `pr-check leaves the owner's agents' caps and claims as they were`.
