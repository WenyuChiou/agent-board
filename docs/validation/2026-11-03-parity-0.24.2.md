## Codex direct delivery

The owner's main question for this run: can the board deliver an answer to a running Codex session?

**What was run.**
- An interactive Codex CLI session was started in the scratch folder, on a synthetic turn that runs `Start-Sleep -Seconds 240` and then asks "Blue or green?". Opening it started Codex's managed app-server daemon.
- While the turn ran, the shipped connector `scripts/codex-message-connector.mjs` got the explicit binding on stdin that MESSAGING.md asks for: the page, the absolute Codex executable, the thread and its active turn.

**Result: not delivered.**
- The connector exited 1 after 5 s with `{"error":"OWNER_TIMEOUT"}`.
- `codex app-server proxy` sent no line in reply to `initialize`. The same silence came from:
  - the CLI's own binary;
  - the CLI with `--sock` set to the daemon's control socket;
  - the daemon's 0.165.0 binary.
- So nothing was attached, and the card offered no Send.

**What the person saw.**
- The card showed `Copy "Blue"`, `Copy "green"` and `Write your own`. Pressing `Copy "Blue"` showed `Working…` for about 2 s, then "✓ Copied to clipboard" and `Copy again`.
- The card showed no Accepted, received or delivered state, and the answer never reached the Codex turn.
- The card stays in Needs you, with no hide button, until the person answers in Codex.

The details are on #557, which tracks this for 0.24.3.
